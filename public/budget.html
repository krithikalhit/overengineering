Assumptions

CSV columns: name, category, entity, currency, gross, gst, tds, other, shape, start, status. Importing replaces the lines in this scenario only.

Budget heads mirror the payee prefixes in FY 26–27 Riffle Payments Planner. Recurring lines are taken from the Aug 26 tab, the most recent complete month; lines that appeared once between Apr and Aug are carried in switched off, so each head shows its full history. Cash out is modelled as gross + GST − TDS + other, which is what actually leaves the account. The planner's own "Burn till" figures are bank-balance deltas and run −$6.0k to +$4.7k against the sum of the line items.
